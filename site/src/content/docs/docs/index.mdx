meteo by Azohra is machinery for publishing soaring forecasts to a flying
community: an engine you point at your launches, and packages that read,
analyze, and draw what it publishes. Choose the outcome closest to
yours.

## Seven starting paths

| You want to… | Start here | You will produce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish for your launches | [Configure launches](/docs/publish/configure-launches/) | Static model manifests and profile JSON on infrastructure you control |
| Integrate with TypeScript | [Render a first Meteogram](/docs/briefing/render-first-meteogram/) | A validated scene graph, self-contained SVG and key, typed findings, or cross-model comparison |
| Show your weather station live | [Station getting started](/docs/station/getting-started/) | A live wind page served from your own station's feed, in React or plain HTML |
| Decode GRIB2 or JPEG 2000 in TypeScript | [GRIB2 in pure TypeScript](/docs/grib/) | Typed grids and values from provider bytes, gated bit-for-bit against ecCodes |
| Contribute to the forecast engine | [Forecast architecture](/docs/forecast/architecture/) | A tested source, contract, builder, scenario, or documentation change |
| Learn the technical ideas | [Reading a Meteogram](/docs/briefing/reading-a-meteogram/) and the [logbook](/logbook/) | A working model of the chart, its units, derivations, and uncertainty |
| Understand the lineage | [Why this project exists](/about/) | Where the platform came from, and the projects it descends from |
