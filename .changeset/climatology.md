The climatology cube: the whole archive as one filterable document. New `StationClimatology` family (`STATION_CLIMATOLOGY_SCHEMA_VERSION` 1, own schema artifact): (month, slot-of-day, sector) **sums** bucketed in the station's standard time, binned with the consumer's thresholds (a judgment parameter with no default, echoed as `thresholdsMps`), calm counted in the bucket and never a sector, empty buckets absent, and a per-year coverage ledger with leading pre-station years trimmed. Pure fold (`createClimatologyAccumulator`/`foldClimatologyPoints`/`accumulatedCells`) and re-aggregators (`climatologyRose`, `climatologyPattern`, `climatologyCoverage`, `climatologyFavorableShare`) make every month/season/time-of-day filter a client-side re-sum — no refetch, proven lossless against `windRose`/`dailyPattern` on the same points. `windRose` itself gains an optional `bandThresholdsMps`, giving each `RoseSector` a `bandCounts` stack. The WindNerd loader (`loadWindnerdClimatology`; fleet-level `loadStationClimatology`) fans out one records request per calendar year at the vendor's own 180-minute period — closed years cached ~30 days, the running year 6 hours, the raw-records cache shared across consumers regardless of thresholds — and fails whole when a year is refused. The handler serves `/climatology?station=` once the host mounts its judgment (`climatology: { thresholds }`); `climatologyEndpoint` and the fetch-once `createStationClimatologyStore` complete the client road. Records parsing now tolerates the real pre-station year shape (sensor columns dropped wholesale on an empty window), verified live against location 240. New Climatology docs page.
